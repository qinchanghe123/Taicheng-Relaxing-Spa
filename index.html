FAQs

Before your visit.

A few simple details to help you feel prepared, comfortable, and confident before your massage.

Please arrive 5–10 minutes early so you have time to check in, choose your service, and settle in without feeling rushed.

If you have a recent injury, serious pain, or a medical condition, please check with your healthcare provider first and tell us before your session begins.

Please call as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel your appointment so we can offer the time to another guest.

Parking is available near the location. Please allow a few extra minutes during busy times.
